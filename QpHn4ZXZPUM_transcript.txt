The historical development of the concept of culture. Let's begin with a simple but important observation. The word culture and its earliest use didn't refer to a specific object, state, or content. The concept of culture is central to cultural studies today, but its origins are found in ancient Latin. The modern word culture comes from Latin root verb kera which means to cultivate, to inhabit, to honor and as a noun it means the act or result of cultivating. Poets and scholars of ancient Rome used it in their treatices letters in the sense of to cultivate something to process or improve it. In classical Latin, the phrase agree cultura or agriculture meant the cultivation of the land. It implied care, protection, and the creation of favorable conditions for growth. In ancient Rome, the word culture was typically used in combination with an object or area of focus, culture of speech, culture of behavior and so on. This use has continued into modern times. Later, especially during the classical and medieval periods, the word culture took on a new meaning.
