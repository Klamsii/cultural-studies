# 📘 Semiotics, Signs, and Symbols in Culture

## 1. What is Semiotics?
Semiotics derives from Greek **semeion**, meaning **"sign"**. It is the science of sign systems and meaning encoding.

---

## 2. Key Semiotic Theories

### 2.1 Ferdinand de Saussure
Saussure proved a sign links a **Signifier** (acoustic image) and **Signified** (mental concept), establishing sign arbitrariness (*l'arbitraire du signe*).

### 2.2 Peirce's Triad (Icon, Index, Symbol)
- **Icon (*Icon*):** Physical resemblance (drawing, *Koshkar Muiz* horn ornament).
- **Index (*Index*):** Direct physical/causal proof (smoke for fire, footprint).
- **Symbol (*Symbol*):** Pure social convention (STOP traffic sign, national flag).

### 2.3 Morris's Semiotic Dimensions
- **Syntactics:** Sign to sign relations.
- **Semantics:** Sign to object relations.
- **Pragmatics:** Sign to interpreter relations.

### 2.4 Vygotsky & Kristeva
Vygotsky demonstrated sign mediation in psychology. Julia Kristeva created **semanalysis (*semanalysis*)** as a critique of meaning production.
