## 🎯 Essence in 60 Seconds
- **Semiotics:** The scientific study of sign systems (from Greek *semeion* — sign).
- **Saussure:** Two-sided sign: Signifier (acoustic image) + Signified (mental concept).
- **Peirce's Triad:**
  1. **Icon:** Visual resemblance (portrait, *Koshkar muiz* ornament).
  2. **Index:** Physical causal connection (smoke indicates fire).
  3. **Symbol:** Arbitrary social convention (STOP sign, flag).
- **Morris (1938):** 3 dimensions of semiotics — Syntactics (sign-sign), Semantics (sign-object), Pragmatics (sign-user).
- **Vygotsky & Kristeva:** Vygotsky proved sign mediation in psychology; Julia Kristeva established **semanalysis** (*semanalysis*).

## 📖 Main Content

### 1. Charles Sanders Peirce's Sign Triad
**Peirce categorized signs based on their relation to reality into Icons, Indexes, and Symbols.**
1. **Icon:** The sign possesses direct visual or physical resemblance to its referent (Kazakh *Koshkar muiz* ornament, portrait, map).
2. **Index:** The sign possesses a direct physical or causal connection to its referent. Smoke is a logical index of fire; footprints on snow indicate a person.
3. **Symbol:** The sign possesses an arbitrary connection based purely on social contract (STOP sign, national flag, alphabetic letter).

### 2. Three Dimensions of Semiotics (Charles Morris, 1938)
**Charles Morris divided semiotics into three functional branches.**
1. **Syntactics:** Studies relations of signs to other signs (grammar, structure).
2. **Semantics:** Studies relations of signs to their referent objects (meaning).
3. **Pragmatics:** Studies relations of signs to human interpreters (user perception).

### 3. Vygotsky & Julia Kristeva's Semanalysis
**Julia Kristeva proposed semanalysis as an alternative critique of meaning.**
Lev Vygotsky demonstrated higher psychological processes develop through sign mediation. Julia Kristeva introduced **semanalysis** (*semanalysis*) — an approach combining semiotics and psychoanalysis to critique sign practices.

## ⚖️ Common Misconceptions

| Concept A | Concept B | Key Difference | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Index** | **Symbol** | Physical causal proof (smoke -> fire) vs arbitrary contract (STOP sign). | Smoke = Index; red octagon STOP = Symbol. |
| **Semantics** | **Pragmatics** | Sign-to-object relation vs sign-to-human user relation. | Dictionary definition = Semantics; reader reaction = Pragmatics. |
