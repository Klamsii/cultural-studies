# 📘 Early Forms of Religious Beliefs

## 1. Edward Tylor's Evolutionary Theory
Sir Edward Burnett Tylor (*Primitive Culture*, 1871) posited that religious beliefs evolved systematically from animism into complex monotheistic systems.

---

## 2. Six Historical Belief Forms

### 2.1 Animism (*Animism*)
Animism is the ancient belief that all nature is living. Early humans believed trees, mountains, rivers, and animals possessed souls or spirits. Tylor explained animism as an attempt to comprehend dreams, visions, and death.

### 2.2 Fetishism (*Fetishism*)
Fetishism involves religious worship of inanimate physical objects (*fetishes*) believed to hold magical protective powers, such as sacred stones or Kazakh **Tumar** amulets.

### 2.3 Totemism (*Totemism*)
Totemism is the belief in sacred blood kinship between a clan and an animal totem. In Kazakh nomadic tradition, venerating the Sacred Steppe Wolf (**Böri**) exemplifies totemism.

### 2.4 Pantheism (*Pantheism*)
Pantheism identifies God directly with nature. God is immanent within the universe, laying the foundation for Tengriism roots: *Tengri* (Sky) and *Jer-Su* (Earth-Water).

### 2.5 Deism (*Deism*)
Enlightenment philosophy (Voltaire, Newton) viewing God as a Clockmaker who designed natural laws but does not intervene through miracles.

### 2.6 Monotheism (*Monotheism*)
Faith in One Transcendent God (Islam, Christianity, Judaism).
