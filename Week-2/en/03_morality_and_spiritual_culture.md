## 📖 Main Content

### 1. The Golden Rule of Morality & Kazakh Ethics
**The Golden Rule serves as the core foundation of human morality.**
Formulation: "Do unto others as you would have them do unto you." In Kazakh culture, this principle is embodied in the proverb: *«Өзіңе тілемейтінді өзгеге тілеме»*.

### 2. Three Key Paradoxes of Moral Culture
1. **Absoluteness vs Context:** "Thou shalt not kill" is absolute, yet turns into military duty during wartime defense.
2. **Conscience vs Social Shame (Uyat):** True morality demands internal conscience, yet culture often enforces norms through external social shame (**Uyat**).
3. **The Trolley Problem:**
   - **Utilitarianism (Bentham/Mill):** Flip the lever to divert the trolley onto one track to save 5 lives at the cost of 1.
   - **Kantian Deontology (Kant):** Forbids flipping the lever, as humans must never be used as mere means, and active intervention turns the actor into a murderer.
