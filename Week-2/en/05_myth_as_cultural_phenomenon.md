# 📘 Myth as a Cultural Phenomenon

## 1. Roland Barthes's Concept of Myth (*Mythologies*, 1957)
Barthes demonstrated that myth is a second-order semiotic system in modern media. Primary denotation becomes the Signifier for secondary ideological connotation. The main function of myth is **naturalizing ideology** — presenting bourgeois values as natural common sense.

---

## 2. Lotman's Semiosphere & Suleimenov
Yuri Lotman formulated the **Semiosphere (*Semiosphere*)** — culture as a dynamic text with codes. Olzhas Suleimenov (*AZ i IA*, 1975) offered a Turkic semiotic analysis of "The Song of Igor's Campaign".

---

## 3. Myth Types & Tamgaly Petroglyphs
Myth types include Cosmogonic (creation), Etiological (nation origins), and Eschatological (end of world). Tamgaly petroglyphs (*Kunhan*) reflect ancient Eurasian solar sky worship (*Tengri*).
