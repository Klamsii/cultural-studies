# 📘 Global Cultural Codes and Cognitive Styles

## 1. What is a Cultural Code?
A Cultural Code is an encoded system of signs, mental stereotypes, values, and semantic matrices through which a society preserves its historical experience, passes it across generations, and decodes incoming information from the world. It shapes national mindset, sets behavioral norms, and constructs worldviews.

---

## 2. Four Evolutionary Stages of Cultural Codes

### 2.1 Preliterate (Traditional) Code
The preliterate code represents the primary historical state of human civilization where knowledge was transmitted without written scripts.

**Mediums:** Spoken word, traditional rituals, body tattoos, ceremonial dances, and mythic epics.

**Transmission Mechanism:** Information is passed through direct physical contact. Elders teach younger generations through oral storytelling (such as Kazakh *zhyrs* and *tolghau*), ritual participation, and practical apprenticeship. Elders, shamans, and epic singers (*akyns* and *zhyraus*) function as living human libraries.

**Cognitive Style:** Thought is auditory, image-driven, emotional, ritualistic, and collective. People perceive meaning through rhythmic speech, mythic metaphors, and shared emotional immersion.

---

### 2.2 Written (Book) Code
Emerged with alphabetic scripts and matured after Gutenberg's 15th-century printing press.

**Mediums:** Alphabet, manuscripts, charters, printed books, and archives.

**Transmission Mechanism:** Writing liberated human memory by storing data in physical libraries. Knowledge passes through individual reading, formal schooling, and study of codified laws and treatises.

**Cognitive Style:** Forms linear-logical, analytical, and individually reflective thinking. Reading requires silence, solitude, and cause-and-effect reasoning.

---

### 2.3 Screen Code (Cinema & TV)
Formed in the 20th century with film, television, and photography.

**Mediums:** Film reels, broadcast TV, photos, and video recordings.

**Transmission Mechanism:** Relies on centralized mass broadcasting (*mass broadcasting*), setting global standards for fashion and politics.

**Cognitive Style:** Visual, sensory, synthetic, and passive, reducing individual imaginative effort while heightening emotional empathy.

---

### 2.4 Digital Code (Interactive Networked Code)
Contemporary stage driven by the Internet, social media, and Artificial Intelligence algorithms.

**Mediums:** Web platforms, hypertext, mobile apps, software code, and virtual spaces.

**Transmission Mechanism:** The author/audience boundary collapses. Users become prosumers (*prosumers*) — creating and consuming content. Information spreads non-linearly via hyperlinks and viral networking.

**Cognitive Style:** Non-linear, clip-thinking, mosaic-like, and multitasking.
