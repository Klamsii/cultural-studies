# Topic 1.5: Digital Culture and Subcultures (EN Notes)

## 1. Participatory Culture (Nina Uzelac 2008)
Digital users actively produce, remix, and share media rather than passively consuming.

## 2. Digital Youth Subcultures
Gaming, fandoms, meme communities. Scholar Chris Barker (Barker, 582) notes modern youth subcultures rely directly on digital delivery platforms.
