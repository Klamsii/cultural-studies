# Topic 1.2: Material vs. Non-Material Culture (EN Notes)

## 1. Cultural Comparison
- **Material Culture:** Physical, tangible objects (yurts, clothing, tools, technology).
- **Non-Material Culture:** Intangible ideas (values, beliefs, laws, norms, traditions).

## 2. Symbolic Meaning: The Kazakh Yurt (*Kiiz ui*)
- *Material:* Wooden lattice, felt covering, crown (*Shanyraq*).
- *Non-Material:* Symbol of cosmic order, family unity, ancestral honor, and hospitality.

## 3. Cultural Lag (William F. Ogburn)
Occurs when material technology evolves rapidly while non-material laws and ethics lag behind.
