## 🎯 Essence in 60 Seconds
- **Linguistic Relativity:** The Sapir-Whorf Hypothesis states that language structure directs human thought and cognitive perception.
- **Lera Boroditsky's Experiments:**
  1. Spatial navigation in Kuuk Thaayorre (absolute cardinal directions instead of egocentric left/right).
  2. Russian EEG response (faster distinction between light blue *goluboy* and dark blue *siniy*).
  3. Grammatical gender of bridges (German *die Brücke* — feminine/elegant vs Spanish *el puente* — masculine/strong).
  4. Memory for blame (English agentive *"John broke"* vs Spanish event-focused *"El florero se rompió"*).
- **Saussurean Semiotics:** A sign unites a Signifier (acoustic/visual form) and a Signified (mental concept). The sign is arbitrary (*l'arbitraire du signe*).

## 📖 Main Content

### 1. Sapir-Whorf Hypothesis & Boroditsky's Experiments
**Language does not merely convey thoughts; it structures cognitive categories.**
- **Soft Version (Linguistic Relativity):** Native language structure directs habitual attention toward specific world features.
- **Lera Boroditsky's Empirical Experiments (TED):**
  - **Space (Kuuk Thaayorre):** Using cardinal directions (North/South) builds an internal mental compass.
  - **Color (Russian Language):** Distinct terms for *goluboy* (light blue) and *siniy* (dark blue) speed up brain EEG distinction.
  - **Grammatical Gender:** Describing a bridge in German (*die Brücke* — feminine: elegant) vs Spanish (*el puente* — masculine: strong).
  - **Blame Memory:** English focuses on agentive blame (*"John broke the vase"*), while Spanish focuses on the event (*"The vase broke"*).

### 2. Ferdinand de Saussure's Structural Semiotics
**A linguistic sign represents a two-sided psychological entity.**
Ferdinand de Saussure in *Course in General Linguistics* (1916) established that a sign unites a **Signifier** (*Signifier* — acoustic image) and a **Signified** (*Signified* — mental concept). The core principle is sign arbitrariness (*l'arbitraire du signe*): the sound "table" has no physical link to the object, relying entirely on social convention.
