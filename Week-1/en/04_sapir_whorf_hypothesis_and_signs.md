## 1. Linguistic Relativity
The structure of a language shapes habitually how speakers perceive and categorize the world.

## 2. Key Evidence
- **Space:** Cardinal directions in Kuuk Thaayorre.
- **Color:** Light/dark blue distinction in Russian.
- **Grammatical Gender:** Bridge as elegant (German) vs. strong (Spanish).
- **Semiotics:** Sign = Signifier + Signified.
