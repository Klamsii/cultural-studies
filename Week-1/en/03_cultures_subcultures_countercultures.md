## 1. Categories of Cultural Groups
- **Dominant Culture:** Mainstream norms of majority.
- **Subculture:** Group sharing distinct norms within society (gamers, K-pop fandoms).
- **Counterculture:** Group actively opposing mainstream societal values (1960s Hippies, Punks).

## 2. Ethnocentrism vs. Relativism
- **Ethnocentrism:** Judging another culture by your own standards.
- **Cultural Relativism:** Evaluating a culture within its own historical context.
