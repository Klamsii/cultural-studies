## 1. Core Definitions

1. **Sir Edward Burnett Tylor (1871):**  
   "That complex whole which includes knowledge, belief, art, law, morals, custom, and any other capabilities and habits acquired by man as a member of society."
   - Culture is **learned and acquired**, not biologically inherited.

2. **UNESCO Declaration (Mexico, 1982):**  
   "Distinctive spiritual, material, intellectual and emotional features characterizing a society... encompassing arts, lifestyles, value systems, traditions and beliefs."

---

## 2. 5 Core Elements of Culture

- **Values:** Abstract ideals defining what is good, right, or desirable.
- **Beliefs:** Specific tenets accepted as true about reality.
- **Norms:** Rules guiding conduct (Folkways and Mores).
- **Symbols:** Signs and objects carrying shared social meaning.
- **Language:** Symbolic system enabling cumulative cultural transmission.

---

## 3. Society vs. Culture

- **Society:** The organized group of people (Hardware).
- **Culture:** The shared blueprint and lifestyle they create (Software).
